Frequently asked questions

How does ExamPrepper generate exam questions?

You enter any exam topic — or paste your own study notes — and the AI researches it and writes a full question set with plausible distractors, an explanation for every answer and a verified source link to read more.

Which question types are supported?

Classic multiple choice, multi-select ("choose TWO"), true/false and fill-in-the-blank. You can mix them per topic, edit any generated question, or write your own.

What is a weak-spot drill?

ExamPrepper tracks every question you answer. The weak-spot drill re-tests exactly the questions you keep getting wrong until you answer each one correctly twice in a row.

Are the mock exams realistic?

Yes — mock exams are timed server-side, answers stay hidden until you submit, and you get a question navigator with flag-for-review, just like real certification exam software. Sessions even survive a browser refresh.

Can I study together with others?

Yes. Share a topic and everyone on your server can practice the same question set and compete on its leaderboard — the question set is generated once and reused.
